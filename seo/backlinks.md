# Backlink Sources: Top 50 High-Quality Places to Build Links

A curated reference of the highest-value sites for acquiring backlinks, organized by tier and category. Sites are ranked by domain authority, editorial trust, and whether they pass SEO equity (dofollow vs. nofollow).

Use this with the `link-building-strategy` skill for outreach scripts and tactical execution.

---

## Tier 1: Editorial & Media (Highest Authority)

These require earned placement — original data, digital PR, or a genuine editorial pitch. No shortcuts. They are the most valuable backlinks you can get.

| # | Site | Type | Dofollow? | Best For |
|---|------|------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | **Wikipedia.org** | Reference / citation | nofollow (but massive trust) | Getting cited as a source in relevant articles |
| 2 | **LinkedIn.com** | Articles / Pulse / Profile | nofollow | Thought leadership, long-form posts, author bio |
| 3 | **Medium.com** | Guest publishing | dofollow | Niche publications, built-in audience |
| 4 | **HubSpot.com** | Guest posts | dofollow | SaaS/marketing niche authority |
| 5 | **Forbes.com** | Contributor program | dofollow | Strict editorial gates, high trust |
| 6 | **BusinessInsider.com** | Digital PR | dofollow | Requires newsworthy data or story |
| 7 | **Newsweek.com** | Digital PR | dofollow | Editorial placement, very high DA |
| 8 | **BBC.com / CNN.com** | Digital PR | dofollow | Major media — earned, not bought |
| 9 | **NBC / CBS / ABC / Fox affiliates** | Localized digital PR | dofollow | Regional angles are easier to pitch |
| 10 | **.edu sites** (Harvard, MIT, etc.) | Resource / broken link replacement | dofollow | Hardest to get, highest trust tier |
| 11 | **.gov sites** | Resource citations | dofollow | Exceptional authority if relevant |

### How to Earn Tier 1 Links
- **Digital PR**: Create original data/surveys, localize it, pitch to journalists. Backlinko case study: 6 campaigns earned 200+ editorial links from Newsweek, Time Out, and local affiliates.
- **Broken link building**: Find broken pages on .edu/.gov sites, offer your content as replacement.
- **The Moving Man Method**: Find expired competitor pages still earning links, suggest your content as replacement. One agency got 311 high-quality backlinks from a single campaign.
- **Guest posting for relevance**: Target the "Citation Core" — sites AI platforms and Google consistently cite in your niche.

|---|------|----|----------|
| 12 | **WordPress.com** | 92 | Free blog with full content control |
| 13 | **Blogger.com** | 98 | Google-owned, instant indexation |
| 14 | **Tumblr.com** | 96 | Microblogging with quick links |
| 15 | **Wix.com** | 95 | Free website builder, profile links |
| 16 | **Weebly.com** | 88 | Owned by Square, reliable |
| 17 | **Google Sites** | 90 | Free, instant indexation, Google trusted |

### Best Practice
Create real content on these platforms — don't just drop a link. Write 500+ word articles that stand on their own. A thin page with one link is less valuable than a substantive article that happens to link to your main site.
