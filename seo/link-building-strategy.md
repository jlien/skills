## Strategy 1: Broken Link Building (The Moving Man Method)

**Best for:** Any site with solid content to offer as a replacement. Works at any stage.

The web changes fast — brands rebrand, pages go dark, services get discontinued. The links pointing to those old URLs stick around. Find them, offer your content as the replacement.

### Step-by-Step Workflow

#### Step 1: Find Outdated Resources
Use a backlink tool (Semrush, Ahrefs, Moz):
1. Enter a competitor's URL into the Backlinks tool
2. Go to the "Indexed Pages" tab
3. Filter for "Broken Pages" — these show all broken pages and how many backlinks each has

**Pro tip:** Don't limit yourself to fully broken links. Dated content — old tool lists, stale stats, discontinued product pages — works just as well. If a competitor's resource hasn't been updated since 2016 but is still earning links, that's an opportunity.

#### Step 2: Find Sites Still Linking to the Old URL
1. From the broken pages list, click the number of backlinks to see all links pointing to the dead page
2. Filter by "Active" to see links that are still live
3. Focus on sites with real traffic and topical relevance
4. Skip anything that looks like a directory or a spam site

#### Step 3: Execute Outreach
Keep the email short. Flag the broken link, identify the specific page it's on, and offer your resource as the fix.

**Winning Outreach Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I was searching for [topic] today and came across your article.

I couldn't help but notice you mentioned [competitor brand/resource] on the page.
[State the reason this is outdated: "I noticed that link isn't working anymore" or "You may have heard, this brand recently went out of business."]

If you want to replace it, I recently published [resource you want to link to].
Might make a nice addition to the page.

Either way, keep up the great work!

[Your Name]
```

**Case study:** SEO agency Nico Digital found a competitor's deleted pricing page still had 3,000+ referring domains. They filtered to 1,109 qualified targets, built one strong replacement page, and sent personalized outreach. **Result: 311 new high-quality backlinks and 1,287 new leads in 90 days.**

## Strategy 2: Digital PR With Localized Data

**Best for:** Teams with the time and skills to create high-quality assets and run full outreach campaigns.

Create a citable asset (original data, survey, or analysis) and pitch it as a story to journalists. Collin Czarnecki of NOBLE Digital Studio calls this the "newsroom mentality" — campaigns built like stories first, SEO assets second.

### The Four Filters Every Journalist Uses
Every campaign that earns high-authority editorial links must pass four filters:
1. **Timeliness** — Is there a news cycle, seasonal moment, or cultural conversation to connect to?
2. **Accuracy** — Is the data verified and credible?
3. **Relevance** — Does the story matter to the journalist's readers?
4. **Urgency** — Why does this need to be covered now?

### How to Build a Campaign

1. **Find your "why now."** Campaigns timed to existing journalistic interest earn coverage faster than those that manufacture interest from scratch.
2. **Collect data with editorial value.** Ask: Would a journalist need this? Does it answer a question their readers are already asking?
3. **Write the pitch like a story lead.** Lead with the finding, not the brand. Journalists need to see: What's the story, why now, why is this source credible?
4. **Target the right outlet for the angle.** A localized data story belongs in a regional newsroom. A consumer survey might lead at a national trade publication.

### Localized Data Approach
Take a broad topic with existing data and break it down by geography or niche. This gives local newsrooms and industry publications a relevant angle they can cover without doing the research themselves.

**Case study:** A travel client launched in late 2024 with 6 campaigns around airfare trends, flight delay patterns, and seasonal travel behavior. Over 18 months, this earned **200+ high-authority media links** from Newsweek, Time Out, and dozens of NBC, CBS, ABC, and Fox affiliates. The site's authority grew from 0 to 29, and referring domains climbed to 800+.

## Strategy 3: Guest Posting for Relevance (Engineering the Citation Core)

**Best for:** Teams ready for the time investment of finding relevant sites and pitching stories.

Skip the "write for us" searches — those find sites hungry for free content, not selective publishers worth pitching.

### The Three-Step Process

#### Step 1: Find the Right Sites
- **Use competitor backlinks:** Pull competitors into Semrush's Backlink Gap tool and filter for editorial placements — posts, roundups, tutorials.
- **Identify your Citation Core:** These are the sites AI platforms consistently cite when answering questions in your niche. A guest post here earns a backlink and a topically relevant mention from a source AI already trusts.

#### Step 2: Pitch With a Story, Not an Article
```
Hi [Editor Name],

I've been reading [publication name] for a while and really enjoyed your recent piece on [topic].

I noticed your readers engage a lot with [related topic], and I have a data-backed angle that I think would resonate:

[1-2 sentence pitch — the finding, not the request]

I'd love to write this up for your audience. No heavy promotion — just original insight they'd find useful.

[Your Name]
[Link to a previous writing sample or portfolio]
```

#### Step 3: Write for the Audience, Not the Link
- Match the publication's tone and depth
- Include original data, personal experience, or case studies
- Place your link contextually where it serves the reader
- Avoid keyword-stuffed anchor text — use natural, branded, or URL anchors

### The Traffic Think Tank Example
A post on Traffic Think Tank covering the best keyword research tools positioned Semrush as a top recommendation. Because Traffic Think Tank already ranked well for relevant searches, the placement built topical association that helped Semrush show up in AI Overviews.

## Strategy 4: Unlinked Brand Mentions

**Best for:** Brands getting talked about online without being linked to.

### Workflow
1. **Find unlinked mentions:** Use Semrush's Brand Monitoring (or Google Alerts + manual search) for `intext:"your brand" -site:yoursite.com`
2. **Prioritize by authority:** Focus on high-DA sites and those relevant to your niche
3. **Outreach template:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for mentioning [Brand Name] in your recent article on [topic]!

If you're open to it, I'd love to offer a quick link to our relevant page at [URL] so your readers can easily find it. It's right in context and would make the mention actionable.

No pressure either way — appreciate you including us.

[Your Name]
```

## Best Practices Across All Strategies

### Do
- ✓ Build a diverse backlink profile across multiple source types (editorial, Web 2.0, document sharing, profiles)
- ✓ Prioritize relevance over DA — a DA 50 link from a topically relevant site is more valuable than a DA 90 link from an unrelated site
- ✓ Use natural, varied anchor text (branded, URL, partial-match, generic)
- ✓ Create genuinely useful content that people want to link to
- ✓ Track everything with a spreadsheet or CRM

### Don't
- ✗ Buy links from PBNs or link farms — Google will de-index or penalize
- ✗ Over-optimize anchor text (exact-match keyword anchors look unnatural)
- ✗ Spam directories or forum profiles with no real participation
- ✗ Guest post on irrelevant sites just for the link
- ✗ Build links too fast — a natural backlink profile grows gradually

### The Authority Signal Stack
Use the `aio` skill alongside this one. High-quality backlinks from editorial sources do double duty:
1. Pass SEO equity (Layer 1 signal)
2. Place your brand in the content that LLMs (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) are most likely to cite

A single high-quality editorial link simultaneously transfers authority, drives referral traffic, places your brand in context for LLMs, and builds trust with new audiences.

### Verification
- Check indexation: `site:targeturl.com` in Google
- Monitor backlink growth in Semrush / Ahrefs / Moz
- Track referring domains, not just total backlinks
- Audit your backlink profile quarterly for toxic links (disavow if needed)
- Measure: does the backlink drive referral traffic? (Google Analytics source/medium report)
