# ABM and Paid Ads — Spend Money Only Where the Account List Says To

Paid in an account-based motion is a precision tool: money goes to accounts already on the list, and the goal is air cover for the outbound motion — not leads from the open market. The moment ads target "everyone like our buyers," this section has stopped being ABM.

## Target-list audiences

Sync the one account list (`icp-and-tam.md`) into each ad platform as a matched audience.

- Upload tier 1+2 (or the segment in focus); refresh the audience on the account-list update schedule so ads follow the list, never the other way around.
- Match rates land at 30-70% depending on platform and list quality — enrich contacts (see `data-and-enrichment.md`) before matching to raise it.
- Exclude customers and current opportunities unless the campaign is explicitly for them. Paying to retell your story to people already in the deal is waste — or worse, noise inside their buying window.

## Promoted team posts

The best organic post of the month (`content.md`) gets budget and runs to the target-list audience.

- Boost the person, not the company page — personal posts carry the credibility; the company page gets the retargeting pixel.
- Judge by the same metric as organic (engagement from target accounts) plus assisted pipeline — not by cheap impressions from the wrong people.

## 1-to-1 ads

The tier-1 play: ads written for one account.

- Trigger: account is tier 1 with a live signal (funding, new leader, hiring — see `signals.md`). The ad references their specific situation the way the outbound first line does.
- Small budgets, hand-built. A 1-to-1 campaign that costs $100 and gets the right exec to notice beats a $2,000 generic campaign aimed at their company.
- Kill rule: no engagement from the account in 2 weeks → archive the campaign and note it in the deal record. Hand-built creative has a maintenance cost; dead campaigns must not linger.

## Intent in the account score

Ad engagement is a signal, same as website visits.

- Pipe ad engagement (clicks, video views from matched accounts) back into the intent score (`scoring.md`) with a modest weight — engagement is weaker intent than a site visit or a reply.
- Never let paid engagement *create* tier-1 status on its own; it can promote, not invent.

## Gifting

Physical gifts for tier-1 accounts at real moments: a new leader arriving, a milestone, after a strong meeting.

- Tied to a signal and a person, not to a calendar ("holiday gift blast" is brand spend, not ABM).
- The follow-up message references the moment, not the gift. The gift is the excuse for the touch; the touch is the motion.

## Retargeting

Site visitors from target accounts get a low-frequency, high-relevance reminder track.

- Creative mirrors what they viewed (pricing visitors see pricing framing; docs visitors see technical framing — same rule as `signals.md` website visitors).
- Frequency cap low (2-4/week). Retargeting that annoys a buyer mid-evaluation costs the deal the campaign was meant to help.

## Search ads

Search is the one place you may catch demand that doesn't know your account list: buyers searching the category or competitor names.

- Bid on: your brand (cheap, protect it), competitor names, and bottom-of-funnel category terms (`content/paid-ads/` covers the broader paid framework — this file stays account-scoped).
- Competitor-term ads route to comparison surfaces, not to the homepage.
- Search traffic from non-ICP companies is fine here — but it routes to self-serve, not to a rep (same rule as `inbound-and-pipeline.md`).

## Decision Framework

- **ABM ads or lead-gen ads?** ABM ads measure account engagement and assisted pipeline; lead-gen ads measure CPL. Different instruments — pick per campaign and say which you're running.
- **Budget up or list down?** List down. Before adding budget, check the audience is fully matched, tiered, and being worked by outbound. Unworked audiences eat budget silently.
- **A campaign can't name its target accounts?** It's not ABM — move it out of this section's budget.

## Collaboration With the Other Skills

- `signals.md` — 1-to-1 triggers and intent weights come from here.
- `content.md` — promoted posts amplify this section's output.
- `content/paid-ads/` — the general paid-ads framework (keyword bidding, testing cadence, CAC vs LTV); ABM-specific rules live in this file.
