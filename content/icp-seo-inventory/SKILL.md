# ICP SEO Inventory

## Overview

Stop publishing 500 blog posts. Most SEO content is noise. The goal is 30-50 high-intent pages that map directly to your Ideal Customer Profile's (ICP) search journey.

## Core Responsibilities

- Identify the ICP's actual search behavior
- Map that behavior to specific high-intent page types
- Prioritize the inventory by conversion potential
- Hand off prioritized topics to `skyscraper-approach` for research and `content-brief` for drafting
- Audit existing content to prune or consolidate into this framework

## The 7 Page Types

### 1. Comparison Pages
- **Format**: [Your Product] vs [Competitor]
- **Intent**: Bottom-of-funnel, choosing between solutions
- **Goal**: Win the head-to-head on specific differentiators
- **Priority**: High (highest conversion)

### 2. Alternative Pages
- **Format**: [Competitor] Alternatives
- **Intent**: Dissatisfied users looking for a switch
- **Goal**: Position as the better option for a specific subset of their users
- **Priority**: High

### 3. Use Case Pages
- **Format**: [Product] for [Industry/Role/Problem]
- **Intent**: "Does this work for me specifically?"
- **Goal**: Prove product-market fit for a niche
- **Priority**: Medium-High

### 4. Free Tool Pages
- **Format**: [Keyword] Calculator / Generator / Checker
- **Intent**: Solving a small problem for free
- **Goal**: Earn backlinks + capture leads via utility
- **Priority**: Medium (high effort, high reward)

### 5. Core Keyword Pages
- **Format**: One definitive page per core category keyword
- **Intent**: Broad discovery + education
- **Goal**: Establish domain authority on the main topic
- **Priority**: Medium

### 6. "How To" Pages
- **Format**: How to solve [Problem Your Product Solves]
- **Intent**: Searching for a solution, not necessarily a product
- **Goal**: Education → Product as the tool
- **Priority**: Medium

### 7. Trust Pages
- **Format**: Pricing, About, Case Studies, Docs
- **Intent**: Validation before purchase
- **Goal**: Remove friction and build credibility
- **Priority**: Essential (pre-requisite)

## Workflow

### Phase 1: ICP Definition
1. Document the specific ICP: role, company size, main pain point
2. List the 3-5 core problems your product solves
3. Identify the top 3-5 competitors they are currently using or considering

### Phase 2: Inventory Mapping
For each ICP segment, map to the 7 page types:
- **Comparison**: [Product] vs [Competitor 1], [Product] vs [Competitor 2]...
- **Alternative**: "[Competitor 1] alternative for [ICP Segment]"
- **Use Case**: "[Product] for [ICP Segment]"
- **Free Tool**: What calculation or check does this ICP need?
- **Core Keywords**: 1-3 main category terms
- **How To**: 3-5 specific problems they solve with your tool
- **Trust**: Pricing, case studies for that segment

### Phase 3: Prioritization
Rank each proposed page by:
- **Conversion Potential** (Comparison > Alternative > Use Case > How To > Core)
- **Competitor Vulnerability** (Can we realistically outrank?)
- **Traffic Potential** (Keyword volume)

### Phase 4: Execution
For each high-priority page:
1. Run `skyscraper-approach` to audit the competitor's best version
2. Create a `content-brief` for the writer/agent
3. Publish and track

## Decision Framework

**Build it if:**
- It matches a specific ICP search query
- You have a genuine competitive advantage to highlight
- The page serves a clear stage of the funnel

**Skip it if:**
- It's a "thought leadership" post with no clear search intent
- You're writing it because "competitors have it" but you can't win
- It requires 500 words of filler to hit a length target

## Collaboration Patterns

- **CPO**: Defines the ICP and core problems
- **ICP-SEO**: Maps the inventory and prioritizes
- **`skyscraper-approach` skill**: Audits competitor content for each page
- **`content-brief` skill**: Structures the final content for AEO/Google
- **Notion**: Stores the inventory as a database with status tracking

## Key Principles

- **30-50 pages, not 500** — concentration of effort beats distribution
- **Match the search, not the ego** — write what they search, not what you want to say
- **Conversion > Traffic** — a comparison page with 100 visits may outconvert a blog post with 10,000
- **One page per intent** — don't bury a use case inside a blog post
